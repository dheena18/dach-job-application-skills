# English cover letter structure

## Length and shape

- One page, 200-350 words, 3-4 paragraphs.
- Same font and header treatment as the resume (see `visual-consistency.md`)
  so the two documents read as one package.
- Date, recipient block, and subject line only where the market expects
  them: include for DACH employers even in English, optional for most
  UK/US applications unless the posting or portal asks for a formal letter.
- Named recipient if it can be found from the posting, LinkedIn, or the
  company site. Otherwise "Dear Hiring Team" or "Dear [Team name] Team".
  "To whom it may concern" reads as not having tried.
- Sign-off: "Kind regards" or "Best regards" for most employers; "Sincerely"
  for very formal US institutions (law, finance, public sector).

## The four paragraphs

1. **Hook.** One or two sentences that could only be written for this
   company and this role: a product they ship, a problem the posting
   names, a launch, a paper, a referral. If there's a direct connection
   (uses the product, knows someone there, worked in the same niche), it
   goes in the first line.
2. **Proof.** Two or three achievements with results, chosen because they
   match the posting's top requirements. Brief context for each — this is
   the argument for why the resume is worth opening, not a summary of it.
   See `persuasion-and-evidence.md` for the CAR framework this should use.
3. **Fit.** Why this company, what about the team or mission or stage is
   attractive, and what the candidate would want to work on — with one
   concrete, checkable detail about the company. Also where the obvious
   question gets answered, if there is one (see `gap-and-weakness-framing.md`).
4. **Close.** Plain and confident: availability, notice period, start date,
   salary expectation if asked. One sentence inviting a conversation. No
   "I would be honoured," no "I am confident I am the perfect candidate."

## Openers that get the letter binned

- "I am writing to express my interest in the position of..."
- "I am excited to apply for..."
- "Please find attached my resume for..."
- "With great enthusiasm..."
- Anything starting "As a passionate..." or "As a [role], I..."

## Openers that work

The shape, not the words — invent the specifics for the real company:

- "Your posting asks for someone who has taken a [X] from prototype to
  production. I did that [N] times at [company], and the [most recent one]
  still [concrete ongoing fact]."
- "I have used [product] for [time], and the [specific flow] you're hiring
  someone to rebuild is the one I run into most."
- "[Name] on your [team] suggested I write to you about the opening."
- "Your recent [paper/post/talk] on [topic] is close to [candidate's own
  relevant work], and the open question it raises is one I'd want to work on."

## Tone by company type

| Company | Tone | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Startup | Direct, conversational | "I've spent three years building exactly this." |
| Corporate / enterprise | Professional, measured | "My experience in enterprise integration matches the objectives in the posting." |
| Public sector, university | Formal, criteria-driven | "I address each of the selection criteria below." |
| Research lab | Precise, references work | Name the paper, the method, the open problem. |
| Agency, creative | Personality forward | "Your rebrand for [X] is why I'm writing." |
| German/DACH employer, English posting | Professional, still fairly measured | Keep the DACH expectations that matter — see `letter-structure-de.md`'s notes on English applications to German companies. |

See `tone-and-culture-calibration.md` before picking a register; don't
default to "startup direct" just because it reads more engaging.

## Template shape

```
Firstname Lastname
City, Country | name@email.com | +xx xxx xxx xxxx

[Date]

Application: [Job title exactly as posted] (Ref [number, if given])

Dear [Ms/Mr Lastname / Hiring Team],

[Hook — one or two sentences unique to this company and role.]

[Proof — 2-3 CAR-framed achievements matched to the posting's top requirements.]

[Fit — why this company/team, one concrete detail about them, and the
obvious question answered here if there is one.]

[Close — availability/notice period/salary if asked, one line inviting a conversation.]

Kind regards,
Firstname Lastname

Attachments: CV[, references, degree certificates if applicable]
```

## Verify list

Before delivering: contact name spelling, company legal name vs. brand
name, job title exactly as written in the posting, reference number, every
number in the letter, the date.
